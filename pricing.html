Simple & Transparent

How booking and payments work.

We believe in absolute clarity and trust. Our payment structure is broken into three simple milestones.

01

25% Deposit

Lock in your wedding date on our studio calendar with a digital agreement and 25% retainer.

02

Timeline Planning

We hold a pre-wedding consultation to map lighting, ritual timings, and special family requests.

03

50% Prior to Wedding

Settle the interim balance one week prior to your wedding day as we finalize crew logistics.

04

25% on Gallery Delivery

The final balance is settled once your curated digital gallery is delivered and approved.

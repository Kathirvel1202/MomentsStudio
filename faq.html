Dates & Reservations

Booking & Availability

How we secure dates and coordinate multi-event schedules across India and destination weddings.

We recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance, especially for auspicious wedding dates between October and March. Because we dedicate our lead crew to only one wedding celebration per weekend, dates fill up quickly on a first-confirmed basis.

Once we finalize your custom proposal or package selection, we issue a written studio agreement outlining deliverables, crew size, and dates. Your booking is 100% locked upon signing the agreement and remitting the advance retainer.

Yes! While our primary studio is based in Bengaluru, we have documented celebrations in Udaipur, Goa, Kerala, Jaipur, Thailand, and Bali. Travel tickets and modest lodging for the crew are billed transparently at actuals with no hidden markups.

Dedicated Date Reservation

We accept only one wedding per weekend so you receive our undivided creative attention and full senior team on your wedding day.

On the Day

Photography & Film Style

Our creative philosophy, gear, and camera presence during sacred moments.

We practice unobtrusive documentary photojournalism. During sacred rituals, we maintain a respectful distance, using telephoto primes and silent shutters so we never block priests, elders, or guests. We capture raw emotion as it unfolds naturally.

Almost all of our couples feel camera-shy at first! We never demand stiff or awkward poses. Instead, we use gentle prompts, guide you toward flattering natural lighting, and encourage candid interactions so your photographs feel warm and effortless.

Yes. In our pre-wedding vision questionnaire, we request a list of key family members, VIP guests, and essential cultural moments. A dedicated crew member ensures these priority combinations are documented seamlessly.

Drone cinematography is included in our Signature and Heirloom packages. However, flights are subject to venue clearance, local DGCA airspace regulations, and weather conditions (heavy rain or severe wind).

Post-Production & Keepsakes

Deliverables & Timelines

From bespoke editorial color correction to fine-art printed heirlooms.

We deliver a 30–50 image preview gallery and a 60-second social reel within 72 hours. The complete color-corrected online gallery is delivered in 45 days, followed by your cinematic highlight film within 60 days.

You receive access to a private, password-protected online gallery hosted on high-speed servers for 12 months. You and your family can download high-resolution print files, web-optimized versions, or order prints directly.

Once you select your favorite frames, our editorial designers curate an elegant story layout. You receive an interactive digital proof for review. Upon approval, albums are printed on archival photographic paper and hand-bound in genuine Italian leather or premium linen.

We do not provide unedited raw files as they are unfinished negative data. Every delivered image is individually culled, color-graded, and cropped to reflect the studio’s signature artistic standard. However, all full-length documentary ceremony footage is delivered in crisp 4K.

Transparent Terms

Payments & Policies

Clear milestones with zero hidden surprises.

Our payment structure is transparent: 25% advance retainer to lock the date, 50% one week prior to the wedding, and the remaining 25% upon delivery of your online gallery.

We understand that unexpected circumstances arise. If you need to reschedule with at least 45 days’ notice, your retainer can be transferred to any available date within 12 months without penalty.

Security is paramount. Every camera records simultaneously to dual SD/CFexpress cards. On the evening of each wedding day, files are backed up to two separate encrypted hard drives and synced to secure off-site cloud storage.

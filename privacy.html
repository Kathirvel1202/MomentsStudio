Studio Transparency

Privacy Policy

At Moments Studio, we understand that wedding photography and cinematography encompass intimate family moments and personal memories. We are dedicated to maintaining the strictest privacy and confidentiality regarding your personal information, communications, and media archives.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact our studio or book wedding coverage, we collect relevant information necessary to plan and deliver your services, including:

  • Contact details: Couple names, phone numbers, email addresses, and postal addresses.
  • Celebration details: Wedding dates, itinerary, venue locations, and family traditions.
  • Creative preferences: Portrait shot lists, soundtrack preferences, and special requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively to prepare customized service proposals, coordinate day-of production logistics, deliver high-resolution media galleries, and communicate milestone updates.

3. Image Privacy & Portfolio Sharing

We take pride in showcasing genuine love stories. However, we deeply respect personal comfort and discretion. We will never share sensitive, private, or dressing moments without explicit written authorization. Couples who require strict non-disclosure or confidential coverage may request a private NDA agreement prior to booking.

4. Gallery Hosting & Data Security

Your online galleries are password-protected with 256-bit SSL encryption. We preserve dual off-site backups of your culled wedding archive for a minimum of 24 months following delivery.

5. Contact Our Privacy Team

For any questions regarding your data or archive storage, please contact us directly at hello@momentsstudio.in or visit our studio in Indiranagar, Bengaluru.

Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Moments Studio. We believe in clear, fair, and professional agreements so couples can enter their wedding celebrations with total peace of mind. These terms govern all wedding photography and cinematography bookings.

1. Booking Confirmation & Retainer

Dates and dedicated crew assignments are confirmed only upon receipt of a signed written studio contract and an advance retainer of 20% of the total contract value. Verbal dates are held as tentative for a maximum of 48 hours.

2. Payment Schedule

Our standard milestone payment schedule is as follows:

  • 20% Retainer: Due upon contract signing to lock your wedding dates.
  • 60% Production Payment: Due 7 days prior to the first event commencement.
  • 20% Final Milestone: Due upon notification of online gallery readiness.

3. Delivery Schedule & Production Stages

Preview images (30–50 photos) and a 60-second social reel are delivered within 72 hours. Complete color-graded online galleries are delivered within 45 days. Cinematic wedding films are finalized within 60 days following music selection. Fine-art physical albums are delivered within 30 days of client album spread sign-off.

4. Rescheduling & Date Adjustments

If you must reschedule your celebration, we will transfer your deposit to any open date within 12 calendar months with at least 45 days’ written notice, subject to lead crew availability.

5. Copyright & Personal Usage Rights

Moments Studio grants the couple unlimited, non-commercial, personal printing and digital sharing rights for lifetime use. Moments Studio retains authorship copyright in accordance with intellectual property laws.
